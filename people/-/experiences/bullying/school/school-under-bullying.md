# School Using Bullying and Bullying Using School

*Copyright (c) 2026 vegahen*

My childhood was happy, but my school turned me into [Ross](/media/tv-series/friends.md). A critical rationalist who - like [Monica](/media/tv-series/friends.md) - lives by the mantra fool me once, shame on me.

Ironically, Ross does not like to watch Friends. So I traumatized myself watching [Malcolm in the Middle](/people/-/media/tv-series/malcolm-in-the-middle.md) on TV, instead of [The Office](/media/tv-series/office.md). The [Ministry of Truth](/people/-/unethical/nrk/nrk.md) just [purchased the rights to The Office](/services/anonymous/nrk-tv/nrk-tv.md) for a couple of years though, so perhaps we should be hopeful for our next generation of incels.

Since there were so few people in my school, I didn't really get to pick my friends. There were two friends and three romantic interests in my class. I don't really know if I picked Monica the first time - the right one - but I think maybe not because she ended up with someone else. Although that may also have been because I picked the wrong [me](/people/-/-.md), though - I don't even think [Sigmund](/psychology/tasks/motorcycling.md) would consider dating your sister a good idea.

I was able to avoid a Three's Company type situation, though. In school, that is - [the rest of the world](/psychology/lies/propaganda.md) comes later.

... as I was reprocessing my childhood bullying trauma, a friend called me up all worried and sent me down some die Gedanken sind frei rabbit hole instead - it's okay though, it's been like twenty years, I'll probably remember whatever I was about to type whenever I have the time to pick up the threads here...

The moment the principal opened their mouth, my bully started crying. That surprised me. I don't remember what I was expecting, because I probably haven't been expecting that other people would react in such an unrealistic way since I was a kid.

My bully told me later - after we had become close friends - that they had felt pressured into doing it by the other kids, and that they were a victim of bullying themself. I realized that bullying can't really be real then, because the bullies tend not to know that they are performing the act.

Hence the illusion of bullying was born in my critical rationalist worldview. This is why critical rationalism is so dangerous. When you try to combine the observations from all the different worldviews around you, you will always end up favoring the observations coming from the stubborn self-centered main characters and the narcissists. Those are the observations you are never able to rationalize in any other way, because those people refuse to [think it possible they may be mistaken](/media/tv-mini-series/ascent-of-man.md).

We need to make sure our kids understand that although critical rationalism works wonders for science in objective reality, your subjective experiences are actually in a completely different realm because they don't satisfy [Karl's criterion](/psychology/epistemology/epistemology.md). Understanding that [everyone lives on a different planet](/psychology/theories/planet-theory.md) might have saved me a decade or two of suppressing my bullying trauma because it wasn't real, instead of processing it.

Even some doctors don't seem to realize that, so I'm guessing my defensive doctor probably has similar experiences with being turned into a critical rationalist by their school.
