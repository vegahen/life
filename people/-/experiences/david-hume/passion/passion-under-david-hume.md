> Phoebe has already answered all of your scientific questions about psychology; perhaps all we needed to fix the sick leave statistics was aromatherapy

This book is my best attempt at aromatherapy. [Karl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Popper) would describe it as unscientific, which is true because it does not satisfy [Karl's criterion](/psychology/epistemology/epistemology.md).

In all seriousness, though, [Phoebe](/media/tv-series/friends.md)'s solution had nothing to do with aromatherapy - that is just an unimportant detail. Phoebe's solution is having the guts to say that [I wish I could, but I don't want to](/democracy/2026/2026-08/drink-or-not-to-drink.md). At some point in her life, she wanted to try aromatherapy, and it [made her feel good](/psychology/stances/why-people-are-guided-toward-this-or-that-explanatory-model.md).

This is the reason we listen to [Sigmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigmund_Freud) instead of Karl - Sigmund did not give a fuck about epistemology, because he cared so much more about people. To state that unscientific theories are not useful at all is to discredit all forms of mental health treatment and the entire field of psychology.

Ironically, this is a field that the critical rationalists seem to have quite a lot of use for, in my own personal experience.

Too bad they are so fucking defensive about it, because they might just learn something. Isn't that what science is all about?
