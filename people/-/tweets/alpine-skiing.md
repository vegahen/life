> My dad thinks I was seven years old during my earliest memory of cross-country skiing. We got caught in some bad weather halfway on a `19km` tour in the mountains, and I remember holding my dad's pole as he pulled me through the wind on our way back. I was twenty-one years old the first time I tried alpine skiing. For my first attempt, my friends brought me to the top of the mountain and down a slope that wasn't really a slope, but the `four meter` wide tracks of a slope prepper machine. I fell down once every `four meters` or so. The following day I got out early and transferred all of my cross-country skiing skills to this new and exotic activity. When my friends joined me after lunch, I went down the hills faster than the lot of them. I can see that you have never fallen and hurt yourself badly, one of them told me.
