> I have always wondered how people who appear to be smart and reasonable can believe in magic or conspiracy theories. During our conversations, I started to understand why: It is because they want to or need to believe it! And that is why discussions are fruitless - it is not a rational belief. This is going to help me understand people a lot better in the future - at work and in private both.

It always makes me feel good when someone can use my ideas for good. I guess we have proven now that my current [explanatory model](/psychology/theories/explanatory-models.md) can be considered [useful](/psychology/epistemology/epistemology.md#my-natural-selection-hypothesis).
