## Discussion

The most amazing thing about Thea's store is that so many things are sold without packaging. You can simply bring your own glass jar, wooden box, paper bag, or whatever other crazy items you want to put her great products in, and then you pay by the kilogram. That is such a great idea, because now we can shop without [trash](/media/shorts/turning-point.md)! How come the regular grocery stores don't do that?
