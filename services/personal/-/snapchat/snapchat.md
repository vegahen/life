When I started working in `2022`, I made a Snapchat account so that I could keep up with my colleagues in our Snapchat group. That only lasted a couple of weeks.

I found the weaponized cognitive dissonance from this app to be superfluous, because the [Tinder](/services/personal/-/tinder/tinder.md) app's [shit](/media/shorts/enshittificator.md) is so much worse. But Tinder also gets me laid occasionally, while Snapchat only tempted me to open the tab filled with shitty reels I could scroll through for thirteen hours straight if I forgot to make plans on a Sunday and never even got out of bed; there was a red notification dot on that tab, so my brain needed to solve that problem and get rid of that red dot.

[Pick your pleasure](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-happiness.md). I chose sex; my screen time didn't suffer as much.
