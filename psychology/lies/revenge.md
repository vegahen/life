## Discussion

When you seek revenge, you interpret the revenge in your own worldview and find that it is just.

When revenge is received, they interpret the revenge in their own worldview and find that it is unfair, disproportionate, and maybe even plain evil. Suddenly you are the dark side, so their side must logically be the light one.
