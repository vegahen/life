# Selective Empathy

> `SSH`: You should not feel empathy with everyone

This is a lie.

If two people feel empathy with each other, their lives will become easier. If you feel empathy with someone, your life will become easier. If you are unable to feel empathy with someone, please see my stance on [details](/psychology/stances/details.md).

If both sides of a conflict feel empathy with each other, killing the other side is no longer a good idea. If only one side feels empathy with the other, the empathic side has an easier time winning the war with their empathic bullets, because soldiers are more willing to fight for an empathic cause than an evil cause, and the empathic side can understand their enemy better. If you are so lucky that your enemy has written a book, you might want to read that book because that could really help you understand your enemy. If you are unable to feel empathy with your enemy, please see my stance on details.

I can agree that

> `SSH`: You should not be kind to everyone
