Although I have flirted with destiny my fair share of times, I decided to stop doing so because I don't got [no time to die](/media/music/no-time-to-die.md) right now.

So if anyone tries to tell you that I happened to die by chance for some unsuspicious reason after speaking my mind, I think it's probably fake news.

I might just be [a bit paranoid](/people/-/tweets/bonfires.md) though; who would do something like that in this day and age?

## Versions

### [Motorcycling](/people/-null/tweets/motorcycling.md)

Empathic feedback can get you a long way; a friend of mine, without missing a beat, [thanked me for becoming an organ donor](/people/-null/tweets/motorcycling.md) as soon as I shared that I was taking driving lessons.

I am too scared anyways to enjoy riding, really. I gave it one last shot a week ago. I was going to write about the experience in my bio, but then a better idea came along, so I didn't have time for that.

I gave away my motorcycle to one of my friends.

### [Tesla](/products/tesla/tesla-highland.md)

My robot car sucks at understanding tasks. While I do sometimes let it operate the gas pedal - because I misunderestimated my speed recently and got my first speeding ticket for going `69km/h` by the way, nice - and, sadly, [the brake pedal](https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-60432351), I NEVER yield control of the steering wheel.

### [Crosswalks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedestrian_crossing)

I understand my tasks: It is my task to make sure that the cars stop so they don't hit me and I die or get injured. It is not my task to act all privileged and YOLO off into the road because it's technically their fault when I get hit.

Learning how to motorcycle is actually a great lesson; it shows you how incompetent we all really are, and why your best bet is to assume that everyone else in traffic is always trying to kill you. There's a good reason why I used motorcycling to [illustrate how to think](/psychology/tasks/motorcycling.md) about tasks.

### [Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defenestration)

I have a crippling fear of heights, so I tend to stay far away from windows. Just wanted to let you know.

### [Enemies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enemy)

I don't have many, thankfully. I got into a knife fight once, but [afterwards I realized](/psychology/theories/general-theory-of-relativity.md) that I didn't need that person in my life anymore. So I believe that the ones I do have are well under control right now.

### [Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health)

Ask my doctor, the vitamin nørd. She can show you my latest bloodwork. Since we last met, I started eating more eggs and got a bit of a suntan, like we agreed.

### [Mental Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_health)

I have actually never felt better about myself. But you can ask the very same doctor, she did quite a thorough assessment when she tried to figure out why my brain suddenly didn't want to sleep more than four hours per day [in two to three intervals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyphasic_sleep).

### [Insomnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insomnia)

This is where I'd place my bet. I think it's because it has so many ideas it needs to write down before it's too late.

## Conclusion

But yeah, I suppose I'm just overreacting thinking you can [upset anyone by writing a book](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/if-those-kids-could-read-theyd-be-very-upset) these days. And if those kids actually do have the attention span to read and understand what I'm trying to say, they'd have to be pretty creative about it to make it believable.

And if they were smart, they would know... [it's not worth it, buddy](/people/-null/tweets/premature-regret.md).

Because becoming a martyr is a great way to spread your ideas even more. But [I'm not that desperate](/people/-/media/music/songs/stayin-alive.md), so please don't go getting any ideas if you happen to agree with me.
