Although I have flirted with destiny a couple of times, I decided to stop doing so because I don't got [no time to die](/media/music/no-time-to-die.md) right now.

I might just be [a bit paranoid](/people/-/tweets/bonfires.md) though; who would do something like that in this day and age?

## Versions

### [Motorcycling](/people/-null/tweets/motorcycling.md)

[Empathic feedback](/people/-null/tweets/motorcycling.md
) can get you a long way; a friend of mine told me that without missing a beat as I shared that I was taking driving lessons.

I am too scared anyways to enjoy riding, really. I gave it one last shot a week ago. I was going to write about the experience in my bio, but then a better idea came along, so I didn't have time for that.

I gave away my motorcycle to one of my friends.

### [Tesla](/products/tesla/tesla-highland.md)

My robot car sucks at understanding tasks. While I do sometimes let it operate the gas pedal - because I misunderestimated the speed I was going recently (`69km/h` by the way, nice) and got my first speeding ticket - and, sadly, [the brake pedal](https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-60432351), I NEVER yield control of the steering wheel.

### [Crosswalks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedestrian_crossing)

I understand my tasks: It is my task to make sure that the car stops so they don't hit me and I die or get injured. It is not my task to act all privileged and YOLO into the road because it was technically their fault.

Learning how to motorcycle is a great lesson; it shows you how incompetent most drivers really are. There's a good reason why I used motorcycling to [illustrate how to think](/psychology/tasks/motorcycling.md) about tasks.

### [Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defenestration)

I have a crippling fear of heights, so I tend to stay far away from windows. Just wanted to let you know.

### [Enemies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enemy)

I don't have many, thankfully. I got into a knife fight once, but [afterwards I realized](/psychology/theories/general-theory-of-relativity.md) that I didn't need that person in my life anymore. So I believe that the ones I do have are well under control right now.

### [Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health)

Ask my doctor, the vitamin nørd. She can show you my latest bloodwork. Since we last met, I started eating more eggs and got a bit of a suntan, like we agreed.

### [Mental Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_health)

I'm surprised you really had to ask. But yeah, you can ask the very same doctor. She did quite a thorough assessment when my brain stopped sleeping suddenly because it had so many ideas it needed to write down.

## Conclusion

But yeah, I suppose I'm just overreacting thinking I can [upset anyone by writing a book](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/if-those-kids-could-read-theyd-be-very-upset) in this day and age. And if those kids CAN read, they'd have to be pretty creative about it to make it believable.

And if they were smart, they would know... [ten points to whoever can tell me where I got this quote from](/people/-null/tweets/premature-regret.md).

Becoming a martyr is a great way to spread your ideas, anyways.
