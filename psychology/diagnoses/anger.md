The hardcore paranoid-schizoid position is characterized by its delusional trauma phantasies and resistance to doublethink.

[This is how](/psychology/stances/federation-internationale-de-football-association.md) you turn a planet into a regiment of ghosts - [obedient ghosts or tortured ghosts](https://youtu.be/ltjI3BXKBgY?t=39).

The hardcore paranoid-schizoid trauma phantasies channel doublethink resistance into the [betrayal of the human spirit](/psychology/values/value-1.md) - the [cognitive dissonance](/psychology/spectra/cognitive-dissonance-spectrum.md) is strong enough to kill.

This is why an angry person will make you [scared](/psychology/diagnoses/fear.md). But you need to embrace that fear, because fear is cognitive dissonance and it tells you that there is something wrong with your [personal boundaries](/psychology/stances/boundaries.md). Either you have been acting like an asshole toward that person, or you have been trusting an asshole you should not have trusted.

Whatever you do, do not respond to fear with anger - [lead](/psychology/diagnoses/defence.md) [you](/psychology/diagnoses/blame.md) [to](/psychology/diagnoses/asshole.md) [the](/psychology/diagnoses/bullying.md) [dark side](/psychology/diagnoses/numbness.md), [it](/media/tv-mini-series/apocalypse-la-1ere-guerre-mondiale.md) [will](/media/tv-mini-series/apocalypse-la-2eme-guerre-mondiale.md).
