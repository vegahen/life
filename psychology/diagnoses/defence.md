# Defence

*Copyright (c) 2026 vegahen*

The defensive position is characterized by its delusional empathy phantasies and resistance to love.

This position is quite unique, because it is actually tied to two different delusions.

If you happen to be an [asshole](/psychology/diagnoses/asshole.md), you are suffering from the [illusion of trauma](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-trauma.md)

If you happen to be [the greatest](/people/-/media/shorts/billie-eilish-hit-me-hard-and-soft-official-trailer2.md), you are suffering from the [illusion of grandeur](/psychology/lies/illusion-of-grandeur.md)

Delusions of grandeur are typically the optional one, although [Keanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) is often considered one of the exceptions.
