The puny paranoid-schizoid position is characterized by its delusional trauma phantasies and avoidance of doublethink.

The puny paranoid-schizoid trauma phantasies channel doublethink avoidance into the [betrayal of friendship](/psychology/values/value-2.md) - the [cognitive dissonance](/psychology/spectra/cognitive-dissonance-spectrum.md) is strong enough to ghost.

This is why ghosting feels like betrayal. But the only other option is fawn - for your friend to betray themself. You do not want that either, it is equivalent to befriending one of [Jacob's ghosts](/psychology/diagnoses/anger.md).
