The mediocre paranoid-schizoid position is characterized by its delusional trauma phantasies and the choice between [resistance](/psychology/diagnoses/anger.md), [avoidance](/psychology/diagnoses/fear.md), or [acceptance](/psychology/diagnoses/numbness.md) of doublethink.

I used to believe that I was the type of person who never gets angry; that is a lie, because my [mathematical gaslighting experience](/people/-/activities/paranoid-schizophrenia/public-health/public-health-under-paranoid-schizophrenia.md) did make me angry.

It's also not true because of the [tasks of motorcycling](/psychology/tasks/motorcycling.md) - I was just the type of person who always strived to improve, by either getting angry with, blaming, or even scared of myself.

Such betrayal of your own human spirit pushes you toward the [depressive position](/psychology/diagnoses/numbness.md). The only way out is to accept that [cognitive dissonance](/psychology/diagnoses/paranoid-schizophrenia.md) is in fact empathic feedback, which makes the depressive position [completely delusional](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-trauma.md).
