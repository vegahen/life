The depressive position is characterized by its delusional trauma phantasies and acceptance of doublethink.

The [cognitive dissonance](/psychology/spectra/cognitive-dissonance-spectrum.md) is caused by the [illusion of guilt](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-guilt.md) or the [illusion of evil](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-evil.md) applied to yourself, since that violates your own [subjective experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subjective_character_of_experience) of you [core](/psychology/values/value-1.md) [values](/psychology/values/value-2.md). Since the cognitive dissonance is constant, it needs to be accepted. It can only be suppressed with pleasure, but never cured. Thus the [illusion of happiness](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-happiness.md) - the chase for dopamine and serotonin to distract yourself from the pain - is born.

If the illusion of guilt is strong enough, it may cause you to [resolve the cognitive dissonance](/psychology/diagnoses/blame.md) by punishing yourself - such a solution is only temporary though, because you did not change your explanatory model; if the illusion of evil is strong enough, it may cause you to [resolve the cognitive dissonance](/psychology/diagnoses/anger.md) by killing yourself - such a solution is permanent. The third option is of course to [embrace the fear of yourself](/psychology/diagnoses/fear.md) and run away from your trauma - that typically causes you to kill the planet, however, by doing such idiotic things as getting on an airplane and burning fossil fuels just because you didn't have the [time](/psychology/stances/time.md) to use a sailboat instead.

I was able to solve this problem for myself by realizing that the brain creates [explanatory models](/psychology/theories/explanatory-models.md) using empathy as the positive feedback loop and trauma as the negative feedback loop. I finally understood that I was not to blame for failing to follow the rational solution for how to be a good person, because such a rational solution does not exist. In the words of [David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Hume), reason is, and ought only to be the slave of the passions. This realization instantly cured my depression, and I started [solving the problems that used to make me depressed](/readme.md) instead of letting them weigh me down.

Fortunately, my problem solving skills are so good that the writing of this book opened up the amazing world of hyperfocus in the form of insomnia-driven mania. So tomorrow I'm hoping to gain my first psychiatrist friend as I sit down to let them evaluate me for any or all versions of the [cognitive dissonance spectrum](/psychology/spectra/cognitive-dissonance-spectrum.md).

I'm just a bit sad that they never [put this much effort](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Simpsons/Season_8#Hurricane_Neddy) into [curing my depression](https://www.nrk.no/artikkel/regjeringens-knefall-for-meta-1526230).
