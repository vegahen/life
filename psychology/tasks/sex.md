# The Tasks of Sex

*Copyright (c) 2026 vegahen*

If you want to have sex with someone, it is your task to read signals in such a way that you are absolutely certain you will not commit sexual assault.

If you fail at your tasks, you might go to prison. So please take your tasks seriously.

If you ever end up in a situation where someone accuses you of sexual assault, it is your task to apologize and seek help - you need to find out how you can adjust your personal boundaries to make sure it never happens again.

No universal ethical rules to sexual boundaries exist - if they did not want to, it is sexual assault, no matter how you try to explain it away. If you purposefully do not teach yourself how to read signals, and use your incompetence to feign ignorance, you are an [asshole](/psychology/diagnoses/asshole.md).

If you do so in such a way that a court of law finds you guilty of sexual assault, you are an asshole, a sex offender, and hopefully a prison inmate so the rest of us can feel safe.

If you are truly lost, you always have the possibility of asking verbally for consent; it's really not that hard.

It is also your task to ensure you are never falsely accused of sexual assault. You do that by sufficiently getting to know the person you want to have sex with before you have sex with them, to make sure they are trustworthy. If you did your best and they still falsely accuse you, you are not the asshole.

If someone wants to have sex with you and you do not want to, it is your task to reject them in any and all ways that you feel comfortable with. Unfortunately, it is impossible to set boundaries for other people, so if you are ever so unlucky to be a victim of sexual assault by one of the aforementioned assholes, we all love you and we all support you - please do not blame yourself! It would be [delusional](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-guilt.md) to blame yourself when you are the victim.

If you are ever so unlucky, it is your task to press charges and talk to your close friends about how it made you feel. If you are paranoid, like me, you will have evidence to support your case. If you do not have evidence, it is your task to press charges anyway. It is not your task to decide whether your rapist is such a huge asshole that the courts will be convinced, that is their task. The courts will not judge you if you have been acting ethically, because you will have plenty of people to back you up on the witness stand.

Remember that we only need to eliminate assholes in order to prevent rape. So the best personal boundary you can set to prevent rape is to [provide empathic feedback](/psychology/lies/selective-empathy.md) to everyone you ever meet - just like [Deeyah](/people/-/media/movies/white-right-meeting-the-enemy.md). It is [not a good idea to bully incels](/media/tv-series/ida-tar-ansvar.md), however - invite them to your parties instead.
