# The Narcissism Spectrum

> `SSH`: Narcissism is a mental health spectrum for malfunctioning [planet theory](/psychology/theories/planet-theory.md)

Politicians who are less creative than [Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift) can go home now.

## Versions

### [Defensive Traits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_mechanism) - [The Illusion of Trauma](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-happiness.md)

> Someone who feels the need to protect their planet from other people is hiding something from themself that they [would not like the world to know about](/people/-/media/movies/invention-of-lying.md)

Corporate culture is very defensive.

### [Critical Rationalism](/psychology/epistemology/epistemology.md) - [The Illusion of Happiness](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-happiness.md)

> Someone who feels the need to find out what is wrong with their rational planet because they feel cognitive dissonance

Corporate culture is [very rational](/psychology/tasks/friendship.md).

### [Main Character Syndrome](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/main_character_syndrome) - [The Illusion of Guilt](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-guilt.md)

> Everyone is the main character of their own life, but a main character is someone who insists that [everyone else moves to their planet](/psychology/theories/)

Corporate culture is very authoritarian.

### [Narcissism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narcissism) - [The Illusion of Evil](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-evil.md)

> Someone who has taught themself to ignore cognitive dissonance on their own planet, hence they never learn anything about personal boundaries

Corporate culture acts a lot like Facebook.
