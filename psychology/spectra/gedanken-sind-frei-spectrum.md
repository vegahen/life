# Die Gedanken sind frei Spektrum

> `SSH`: Neuroticism is the usurper of der Thron der freien Gedanken

My best friend's voice was constantly in my head telling me that my every act of kindness toward another human being was actually a manipulative grandiose gesture straight outta the [dark triad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_triad).

Wait, did I just discover hearing voices too lol... I guess now I know how close I have actually been to full-blown paranoid schizophrenia - luckily I was still a critical rationalist the whole time back then.
