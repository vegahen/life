# Die Gedanken sind frei Spektrum

> `SSH`: Neuroticism, obsessive–compulsive disorder, or something like that is the usurper of der Thron der freien Gedanken

My best friend's voice was constantly in my head telling me that my every act of kindness toward another human being was actually a manipulative grandiose gesture straight outta the [dark triad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_triad).

Wait, did I just discover hearing voices too lol... I guess now I know how close I have actually been to paranoid schizophrenia - luckily I was still a critical rationalist the whole time back then, because it wasn't until I became a passionate person whose thoughts are free that I started experimenting with mania, delusional disorder, and paranoid schizophrenia.
