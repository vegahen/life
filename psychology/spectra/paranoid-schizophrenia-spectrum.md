## Versions

### [Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder) - [The Illusion of Happiness](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-happiness.md)

Eliminating cognitive dissonance is always the highest priority, so it's difficult to focus on the less important things.

### [Mania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipolar_disorder) - [The Illusion of Guilt](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-guilt.md)

Kicking doublethink ass is now so important that my brain runs along problem-solving at two hundred kilometers per hour; haven't yet understood why I need to be bipolar just because I used to be a critical rationalist before I became happy... but I guess we'll find out if I ever become a critical rationalist again.

### [Paranoid Schizophrenia](/psychology/diagnoses/paranoid-schizophrenia.md) - [The Illusion of Evil](/psychology/theories/illusion-of-evil.md)

Never thought a scientist could become such a great artiste... or it could be that I was stuck at the previous level the whole time, and this level actually requires a rational break with reality - not just an epistemological or emotional one.
