I'm a big fan of [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com), because their [ads](/psychology/stances/ads.md) are based on consent.

I never consent to ads, which is why I always use [Brave](/services/anonymous/brave/brave.md) when I want to watch something.

Sometimes I use [yt-dlp](/services/anonymous/yt-dlp/yt-dlp.md) instead, so that I can add subtitles to movies or watch videos offline, in addition to removing the ads of course.

It's funny how [free and open-source software](/psychology/stances/free-and-open-source.md) tends to fix problems created by all the other software.

It's even funnier how the people who create problems insist on calling themselves [capitalists](/psychology/stances/capitalism.md).

If you were thinking about consent between humans, not humans and machines, you should see my [tasks of friendship](/psychology/tasks/friendship.md) instead. Machines do not understand tasks, unfortunately, so it's easier if you just take this stance instead whenever you're dealing with machines.
