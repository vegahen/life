# My Stance on Enshittification

```yaml
type: source
urls: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshittification
```

[This](/media/shorts/enshittificator.md) is an unethical tactic used by late-stage capitalists because it [makes humans suffer](/psychology/values/value-2.md).

Human suffering is characterized by an upswing of emotions. Experiencing strong emotions when using a service makes the brain more conscious of that service, for better or worse.

The service now presents a problem that your brain wants to solve, because humans enjoy solving problems. But since the service is not [free and open-source](/psychology/stances/free-and-open-source.md), you are not allowed to solve it, and neither is a [random person in Nebraska](https://xkcd.com/2347). So the brain gets stuck in a perpetual loop of wanting to solve an unfixable problem.

I think that does something to the human brain.
