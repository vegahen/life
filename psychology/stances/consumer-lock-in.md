Consumer lock-in is an unethical tactic used by late-stage capitalists because it hinders competition.

Although I speak Norwegian, I am able to talk to my German friend in English. But they use [WhatsApp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WhatsApp) instead of [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook), so I have to travel to Germany if I wish to communicate.

English is an elegant solution to the physical communication problem, but I'm sure there exists an equally elegant solution to the digital one.

So why did [Meta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Platforms) spend tens of billions buying the messaging platform, when they could instead have spent tens of millions implementing cross-platform messaging support?

And why can I still not consensually message my German friend from [Messenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Messenger) when they own both platforms?
