# My Stance on Details

Details should be gathered until you find an explanatory model you like that fits with all your details. Afterwards, you may stop wasting your time with details if you want and focus on living your life instead.

If you find yourself wondering whether your model is correct, you may keep gathering details if you want.

If your model was not good enough for you, it will break some day and you should try to find a better one.

I no longer care about details, you should look them up on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia) instead of asking me; have you remembered to [donate to Wikipedia](https://donate.wikipedia.org) this year by the way? Or ask a free and open-source AI model to summarize the internet for you so you don't waste your time looking elsewhere, because there is too much information on the internet. Sometimes the AI model will tell you about a book, and you may read that book if you want.

But feel free to ask me just in case, because you might just end up breaking my model. And always feel free to ask your friends, because they might just love talking about those details, and wasting time with friends is never a waste of time. They might even lend you a good book.

Most importantly, if selective empathy is part of your explanatory model, I urge you to gather more details. Inviting your enemies to dinner is a good start.
