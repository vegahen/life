Capitalism is when you can make money on great ideas.

If you have a great idea, you can sell a great product. And many people will buy it, because it is great and it solves a problem perfectly. And [human beings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human) like it when problems are solved. Especially when they are solved perfectly.

Not to be confused with [late-stage capitalism](/psychology/stances/late-stage-capitalism.md), which violates the [natural selection](/psychology/stances/natural-selection.md) of [great ideas](/psychology/stances/great-ideas.md) and [makes humans suffer](/psychology/values/value-2.md), among other unethical side-effects.

In late-stage capitalism, money has become the goal instead of the great ideas. The products that make a lot of money can instead be the shitty products sold by companies that already have a lot of money and are willing to spend it [injecting shitty ideas into your head](/psychology/stances/ads.md).

Late-stage capitalists do not care so much about solving problems, let alone solving them perfectly. And human beings do not like it when [problems remain unsolved](/psychology/stances/consumer-lock-in.md). It's called cognitive dissonance, and it's almost as if the late-stage capitalists have figured out how to [weaponize](/media/shorts/enshittificator.md) [it](/media/books/nineteen-eighty-four.md) [lately](/media/movies/1984.md) because they want us to [stop thinking](/services/personal/-/openai/openai.md).

They don't care because they care more about the money. Sometimes they even [introduce problems](/psychology/stances/enshittification.md) in order to make more money.

So the next time you need to decide how you should vote with your money, ask yourself whether they actually care about solving your problem. In my experience, once I have given them my money they often seem more interested in making me go away than doing so with my problems.

And ask yourself how much of your money will be funneled into [unethical nonconsenual cognitive abuse](/psychology/stances/consent.md).
