> `SSH`: There are three types of people in the world: those who are rational, and those who are irrational

Once all the other theories are understood, we can start communicating with people and make some friends.

If you are a philosopher, you are on your own. You wouldn't want to listen to what I'm about to say anyways. In fact, I also believe that your theories are a lot better than mine.

If you are not a philosopher, it's a good idea to make sure that you both agree on the tasks of friendship first. It will make your life a lot easier. You can [take a look at mine](/psychology/tasks/friendship.md) if you want.

## Versions

Rational people are people you can reason with. Irrational people are people you cannot.

The word irrational actually stems from the root irr, which is Norwegian slang for irritating, and rational. So if you try to reason with an irrational person, you'll only end up becoming annoyed.

And if you try to tell them that they are being irrational, they will counter that they are in fact the rational party; YOU are the irrational one.

Hence there are actually only two types of people in the world: those who are rational, those who are irrational, and those who are passionate.

You'll find the rational people inside your own echo chamber. You'll find the irrational people in other echo chambers. And you'll find the passionate people outside, enjoying life.

I spent most of my life befriending irrational people, and it ruined my life. Now I spend most of my life befriending irrational people instead, and it works perfectly fine because [I don't care about details anymore](/psychology/stances/details.md).
